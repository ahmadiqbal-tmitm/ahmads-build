Privacy

What happens to your email when you sign up on ahmads.build. Short version: it's used to send you new agents and posts, and nothing else.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • The date you signed up, and which page you signed up on.

That's all. No tracking cookies and no advertising tools.

Why

To unlock the agents and posts on this site, and to email you when a new agent or post goes live. We won't sell or share your email.

Where it's kept

In a private Google Sheet that only Ahmad Iqbal can open. Your browser also remembers that you've signed up, so you're not asked again. That note stays on your device, and you can clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Removing your email

Ask any time and your email will be deleted: email ahmiq87@gmail.com or message Ahmad on LinkedIn.
